# 一口日記 (One-sentence diary)

A private, on-device diary: each day the user keeps one short sentence about it, and the app helps them keep the habit going without pressure.

## Language

### Writing

**Entry** (記録):
The one sentence, at most 140 characters with an emoji counting as one, kept for a single calendar day. A day has at most one Entry, and only today or a past day can have one.
_Avoid_: post, note, log, diary (for a single day's text)

**Backfill** (後から書く):
Writing or editing the Entry for a past day on a later day. A backfilled Entry is an ordinary Entry.
_Avoid_: late entry, catch-up

**Prompt** (問いかけ):
The question of the day shown in place of an empty Entry to help the user start writing. It is never saved as part of the Entry.
_Avoid_: hint, placeholder, topic

### Habit

**Streak** (連続記録):
The number of consecutive days, ending today (or yesterday while today has no Entry yet), that each have an Entry, regardless of when those Entries were written.
_Avoid_: chain, run, 継続日数

**Streak repair** (連続記録を取り戻す):
Backfilling the missing day(s) so that a broken Streak counts again.
_Avoid_: streak freeze, restore purchase

**Milestone** (節目):
A Streak length that earns a full celebration: 3, 7, 14, 30, 50, 100, 200 and 365 days, then every multiple of 100 from 400 (400, 500, 600, …). A Streak brought back by importing a backup counts as already celebrated.
_Avoid_: achievement, badge, level

**Longest streak** (最長記録):
The greatest number of consecutive days with an Entry anywhere in the user's history, including the current Streak.
_Avoid_: personal best, high score, best streak

**Reminder** (リマインダー):
A notification, at the user's chosen time, on a day that has no Entry yet.
_Avoid_: alert, nudge, push
